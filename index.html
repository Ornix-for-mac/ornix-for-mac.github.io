Ornix for Mac

Ornix quietly organizes your Mac's files and downloads, keeping everything in its place—reliably and in sync with how you work. Set it once, and it handles the rest.

Automatic Organization

Files sort instantly upon download into smart categories: Images, Videos, Audio, Documents, Compressed, Installers, Folders, and Other. No manual effort required.

Menu Bar Simplicity

Runs unobtrusively from your menu bar. Native SwiftUI build ensures seamless macOS integration—lightweight, fast, and always ready.

Action Window Control

Track every move with a clean interface. One-click options for Undo, Open File, or Open Folder keep you in command.

Pro Features Included

Unlimited watched folders, date-based subfolders (year or month), auto conflict resolution, and instant Organize Now cleanup. Full power, no limits.

Launch at Login

Starts automatically with your Mac. Always on, never intrusive—designed for effortless daily use.

How It Works

1. Set Your Folders

Select Downloads or any folder to watch. Ornix monitors silently in the background.

2. Files Arrive, Get Sorted

New downloads automatically move to the right category. Handles conflicts with your preferred rules: Keep Both, Replace, or Skip.

3. Review and Refine

Open the Action Window to see moves, undo if needed, or trigger a full cleanup anytime.

4. Stays Perfect

Launch at login keeps it running. Pro features ensure subfolders and unlimited monitoring scale with your needs.

FAQ

Is Ornix safe for my files?

Yes. Ornix only moves files within your chosen folders. Full Undo and previews ensure nothing is lost.

Does it work on macOS Sequoia?

Fully compatible with macOS 14 and later. Built natively with SwiftUI for optimal performance.

What about duplicates?

Smart resolution: Keep Both, Replace, or Skip. Pro auto-rules handle repeats consistently.

Can I watch multiple folders?

Unlimited folders supported. Organize Downloads, Desktop, or anywhere you choose.

How do I install?

This is the fully activated version of Ornix 1.3.0. Drag to Applications and launch. No purchase needed—Pro features enabled.
